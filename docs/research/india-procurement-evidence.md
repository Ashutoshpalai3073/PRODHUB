## 1. The legal basis for waiving turnover & prior experience

### GFR 2017, Rule 173(i) ✅ PRIMARY — *verbatim*
> "The condition of prior turnover and prior experience may be relaxed for Startups (as
> defined by Department of Industrial Policy and Promotion) subject to meeting of quality &
> technical specifications and making suitable provisions in the bidding document."

### GFR 2017, Rule 170(i) ✅ PRIMARY
Bid Security (EMD) need not be obtained from **"Startups as recognized by Department for
Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT)"**. Rule 170(iii) permits a **Bid Securing
Declaration** in place of bid security.

### Enabling instrument ✅ PRIMARY
**Ministry of Finance, Dept. of Expenditure, OM No. F.20/2/2014-PPD(Pt.), dated 25.07.2016** —
*"all Central Ministries / Departments may relax condition of prior turnover and prior
experience in public procurement to all Startups (whether MSEs or otherwise) subject to
meeting of quality and technical specifications."*

> ⚠️ **Known discrepancy:** the GFR footnote for the *EMD* exemption cites the same file
> number dated **25.07.2017**. Only the 2016 OM was verified. Cite safely as:
> *"EMD exemption for DPIIT-recognised startups, inserted into GFR 2017 Rule 170(i) vide DoE
> OM F.20/2/2014-PPD(Pt.)"* — without asserting a year.

### 🔑 THE CENTRAL GAP — *this is Sanyog's core argument*
The relaxations are **enabling, not mandatory**. The rule says *"**may** be relaxed"* and only
applies where the buyer made *"suitable provisions in the bidding document."* A startup can
therefore only claim the relief **if that specific officer chose to write it in.**

**So the barrier is not the absence of a rule — it is that exercising the rule is
discretionary, unsupported, and personally risky for the officer.** Sanyog's answer is to make
the relaxation the *default path* inside a templated, fully audited workflow, so the officer
is never the one improvising.

## 2. Maharashtra — the problem statement's own context

> ⚠️ **CORRECTED.** An earlier draft of this file said "₹15 lakh / 24 winners". **That is the
> 2018 figure and is now stale.** The **Policy 2025 raises it to ₹25 lakh and 50 winners.**
> MSInS issued this problem statement — quoting their superseded numbers back at them would be
> the single most damaging error in the submission.

### The current instrument ✅ PRIMARY
**Maharashtra Startup, Entrepreneurship and Innovation Policy 2025**, Annexure-A to **GR No.
कौविऊ-2017/प्र.क्र.243/कौशल्य-2, dated 04/09/2025**, Dept. of Skills, Employment,
Entrepreneurship & Innovation.

**§13.7 Maharashtra Startup Week — verbatim:**
> "a) **Startup Selection:** Each year, a rigorous selection process will identify **50 promising
> startups**…
> b) **Government Work Orders:** The selected startups will be awarded with direct work orders to
> roll out pilots of their innovations in various government departments, agencies, and
> institutions **up to Rs. 25 lakhs**…
> **c) Industry Department will modify procurement guidelines to facilitate procurement from
> startup week winners at state and national level.**"

### 🎯 Clause (c) is the single strongest hook in the entire evidence base
**The Government of Maharashtra has committed, in a Government Resolution, to modifying
procurement guidelines so Startup Week winners can be procured from at scale — and has not built
the mechanism to do it.** Sanyog *is* that mechanism, described in their own words.

### Other 2025 Policy provisions that are literally Sanyog's architecture ✅ PRIMARY
- **§9.6 Sandbox Support** — *"A sandbox environment will be developed… a secure, controlled
  space that allows emerging technologies… to be tested in compliance with government standards…
  enabling startups to validate and refine their innovations under real-world conditions with
  reduced regulatory risk."* **Committed, not yet built.**
- **§8.5 Government Open Data Sets** — a mechanism to open curated datasets to startups, plus
  hackathons/grand challenges using them.
- **§6 Governance** — *"**Each department in the state will provide 0.5% of its outlay** for
  promotion of Entrepreneurship and Innovation."* General Body chaired by the **Chief Minister**;
  **MSInS is the nodal authority** and *"All Departments will bring their proposals related to
  startups, innovation, or emerging technologies through MSInS."*
- **§13.9 Grand Challenge** — winners get an initial pilot grant *"and a **follow-on funding may
  be made available for the deployment** of the solution by the beneficiary department."*

### The original design (2018 GR — still the operative process detail) ✅ PRIMARY
GR dated **27 June 2018**. Funnel: applications → scored **out of 100** → **100 finalists** →
2-day boot camp → 5-day event, **15-minute pitches**, panels of ≥7 incl. ≥2 from GoM →
**24 winners** at **≤₹15 lakh** each, total outlay **≤₹5 crore**. **Pilot 6–12 months**, with
*"access to personnel, data and other means of handholding."* Must have an **MVP** —
*"Idea-stage ventures shall not be considered."* **≥50% of winners must be Maharashtra-registered.**

**The 2018 GR's evaluation matrix is a ready-made scoring rubric** (1–5, ×2 = double-weighted):
Unique Value Proposition — *Competitive Market Advantage ×2, Problem Identification ×2*;
Leadership Team — *academic background, professional experience, team credentials, investor/mentor
credentials*; Social Impact — *Employment Generation ×2, environmental sustainability, living
standards*; Technology Advantage — *Proprietary Technology ×2, innovative technology, IPR*;
Sustainability & Scalability — *Past Successes ×2, financial sustainability, operational scalability*.

### 🔴 Documented friction — evidence-based, and defensible
1. **Cadence collapse.** The 2018 GR mandates the Week **annually in February**. MSInS's own
   winner database shows editions in 2018 (24), 2019 (24), 2020 (24), 2021 (50), 2022 (30),
   **no 2023, no 2024**, 2025 (24). *Derived from their own published data — checkable.*
2. **The state's own purchase manual ignores startups entirely.** The **Maharashtra Government
   Purchase Manual (GR dated 1 December 2016, 141 pp.)** contains **0 hits** for "Startup",
   "GeM" and "MSME".
3. **The state exemption is narrower than the central one.** State Startup Policy 2018 §1.3
   exempts *"Start-ups **(in the manufacturing sector)**"* which *"should have their own
   **manufacturing facility** in India"* — ⇒ **software and services startups get no state
   exemption at all.** The promised separate procurement policy still has not appeared; §13.7(c)
   re-promises it seven years later.
4. **No published outcomes.** MSInS publishes no cumulative work-order value and no pilot→scale
   conversion data. ❌ **Do not compute or quote a cumulative figure — none exists.**

**Scale context** ✅: **29,147 DPIIT-recognised startups in Maharashtra (31 May 2025) = 18% of
India's total, the highest of any state.** Policy targets 50,000 startups over 5 years.
⚠️ The widely-reported **"₹500 crore"** is tied specifically to **§10.1 Chief Minister Mahafund**,
not a whole-of-policy outlay. Judges will know the difference.

## 3. State mechanisms worth borrowing from

### Telangana — G.O.Ms.No.08, ITE&C, 28.04.2018 ✅ PRIMARY
India's closest thing to an SBRI-style procurement sandbox.
- Startup submits an **EoI directly by email, irrespective of any floated tender**.
- A **Quality Evaluation Committee** (ITE&C + line department) evaluates.
- ⭐ **Deemed-approval clock #1:** QEC must report **within 30 days**, *"failing which the
  solution will be deemed to be certified automatically."*
- Certified solutions **deploy a pilot as Proof of Concept** at a designated location.
- ⭐ **Deemed-approval clock #2:** QEC impact study due **within 30 days of pilot completion**,
  *"failing which the pilot will be deemed to be approved for scale up automatically."*
- **15% price preference:** a startup quoting ₹X is evaluated at **₹X × 85/100**.
- Non-unique solutions route to a **Swiss Challenge** (a real design tension — the startup that
  did the discovery work can be outbid by a copier).
- No value cap, no fixed pilot duration.

### Odisha ✅ PRIMARY
- Direct procurement **up to ₹10 lakh**; **limited tender up to ₹50 lakh**, floated **among
  recognised startups only** (a ring-fenced market, stronger than a preference).
- **10% of total public procurement reserved** for startups, *in addition to* the 20% MSME
  reservation (MSME Dept. notification 1696, 13.03.2018).
- EMD exemption + **performance security at 25%** of normal value.
- ⭐ **The best scale-up gate found anywhere:** commercial orders after a successful pilot are
  *"limited to ₹1 Crore at the beginning [and] can be extended further depending upon the
  satisfactory reports from **three or more Government clients**."*

### Kerala — GO(Ms) No 2/2022/SPD, 05.07.2022 ✅ PRIMARY
- **Direct purchase up to ₹50 lakh (excl. GST) with *no tendering process at all*** from
  KSUM-registered startups. (Raised from ₹20 lakh.)
- Live portal metrics: **141 startups · 147 departments · 126 direct procurements · 264 work
  orders · ₹28.05 crore** total work-order value.
- Three mechanisms: **Direct Procurement**, **Department Facilitation** (Demand Day / Demo Day),
  **Innovation Zones** inside departments.
- 🟡 A **₹3 crore** overall ceiling was reported in May 2023 — **GO number unverified.**

### Rajasthan — iStart Innovation Challenge ✅ PRIMARY
⭐ **The most precise pilot payment schedule found.** "T" = work-order issue date:

| Milestone | Timing | Payment |
|---|---|---|
| Inception Report submitted | T+15d | 10% |
| Inception Report signed off | T+30d | 20% |
| First draft presented | T+60d | 20% |
| **User Acceptance Testing** completed | T+75d | 10% |
| **Hand-over** | T+90d | 20% |
| Monthly maintenance (4 months) | T+90 → T+210d | 20% |

- ⚠️ **IPR vests in DoIT&C** — materially unfavourable to the startup. **Sanyog deliberately
  takes the opposite position** (startup retains IP, department gets a licence), per the
  problem statement.
- "Time is of the essence"; delay attracts liquidated damages; committee may extend or withdraw.

### Karnataka ✅ policy PRIMARY
- **Karnataka Innovation Authority Act, 2020** creates **statutory Regulatory Sandboxes** —
  *"a small window of exemption for a prescribed period"* under a regulator's oversight.
- Reimburses **50% of processing fees** for sandbox operators supporting ≥5 startups, and
  **75% of application fees** for participating startups.
- 🟡 KTPP Amendment Rules 2021 (G.O. FD 218 Exp-12/2021) — obtained but is a **scanned image
  with no extractable text**. ₹50 lakh limited tender / EMD waiver / 3-year empanelment are
  **SECONDARY only**; the preference percentage is **unknown**.

### Uttar Pradesh 🟡
OM No. 20/2021/1398/78-2-2021/135 IT/2016, 13.09.2021 — turnover, experience and EMD
exemptions, worded near-identically to Telangana's GO, **but with no pilot route, no evaluation
committee, no deemed-approval clock and no price preference.** UP took the exemptions and left
the sandbox behind.

## 4. GeM — thresholds and Startup Runway

> ⚠️ **TWO CORRECTIONS, BOTH IMPORTANT.**
> **(a) The problem statement's "GeM Green Channel" does not exist.** There is no such thing on
> GeM. "Green Channel Status" is a **Ministry of Defence** policy exempting qualifying firms from
> pre-dispatch inspection — a different scheme in a different ministry. **The innovation route on
> GeM is Startup Runway.** Say so; quietly correcting the brief demonstrates rigour.
> **(b) The GeM thresholds changed in July 2024.** Older write-ups say ₹25,000 / ₹5 lakh.

### Current thresholds — GFR **Rule 149**, as amended by **DoE OM F.1/3/2024-PPD dated 10.07.2024** ✅ PRIMARY

| Value | Route |
|---|---|
| **Up to ₹50,000** | Direct purchase from any GeM supplier meeting quality/spec/delivery — **no comparison required** |
| **₹50,000 – ₹10,00,000** | GeM seller with the lowest price among **at least three different manufacturers** |
| **Above ₹10,00,000** | **Mandatory online bidding or reverse auction** on GeM |

Related (all ✅ PRIMARY): **Rule 161(i)** advertised tender at **₹50 lakh and above**;
**Rule 162(i)** limited tender up to ₹50 lakh; **Rule 160** e-procurement mandatory.
**Rule 171** performance security **3–5%** — **no startup or MSE exemption exists**, a real
working-capital barrier worth surfacing honestly. **Rule 172(1)** advance payment to a private
firm capped at **30% of contract value** — *this is the legal headroom for a mobilisation tranche.*

### GeM Startup Runway 🟡 SECONDARY (GeM's own pages are a JS SPA)
- Launched **November 2019** under the **SWAYATT** umbrella. Only **DPIIT-recognised** startups;
  they carry a **#StartupIndia badge** and get **automatic exemption from prior experience, prior
  turnover and EMD** — applied **at platform level** rather than tender-by-tender. That platform-
  level default is the substantive innovation, and it is exactly Sanyog's design principle.
- Lets startups list **innovative, non-standard products with minimal technical specifications**
  across 14 subsectors — solving "no GeM category exists for my product".
- **Trial orders:** ~15 days (products), ~8–16 weeks (services); needs ratings from **≥3
  government buyers** before wider listing.
- Scale (April 2024): **23,332 startups; 2.84 lakh+ orders; ₹22,954 crore GMV**.
- **GeM payment terms:** due **within 10 days of CRAC** generation. ⚠️ Cite as GeM's stated GTC
  position; do not cite a clause number, and note no penal-interest provision was found.

## 4b. 🎯 The numbers that frame the problem

Use these on slides. Each is traceable to a government or peer-reviewed source.

### The valley of death, quantified — iDEX
From two official MoD figures (PIB "The Defence Decade", 17 Jun 2026; MoD statement 19 Mar 2026):
**676 innovators engaged · 548 design-and-development contracts signed · 58 prototypes cleared for
procurement (~₹3,853 cr) · but only 45 procurement contracts actually signed (~₹2,326 cr).**

> **≈ 8% of development contracts became production contracts.** Present this as *your own
> derivation from two official figures*, not as an official statistic. This single number is the
> problem statement in one line — India's best-funded innovation-procurement programme converts
> roughly one pilot in twelve.

iDEX money for reference ✅: **SPARK up to ₹1.5 crore**, **iDEX Prime up to ₹10 crore**,
**ADITI up to ₹25 crore** (launched 4 Mar 2024, ₹750 cr corpus). **All IP remains with the
startup** — MoD's benefit is an *option to procure* (confirmed by a published corrigendum).

**Documented iDEX friction** 🟡 (Outlook Business, 30 Jun 2025) — quotable and human:
products don't auto-convert to orders; **nodal officers transfer out and institutional memory is
lost** (*"the new officer just didn't have the same level of interest"*); no spiral development;
post-award **bank guarantees equal to order value** on startups that cannot fund them.

### Delayed payments — and who is doing the delaying
- **₹7.34 lakh crore** locked up in delayed payments to MSMEs (**March 2024**, *Delayed Payments
  Report 3.0*, GAME/FISME/C2FO) — *"over 4.6% of GVA"*, affecting **6.4 crore MSMEs**, with
  ***"nearly 40 per cent … owed by government agencies and public sector undertakings."***
  ⚠️ The widely-quoted **₹10.7 lakh crore is the 2022 figure on FY21 data.** Always attach the year.
- 🎯 **"In 2020, 69% of payments by public administration entities were delayed by 60 days beyond
  the due date."** (GAME 2022) — the sharpest government-buyer statistic available.
- **Government has paid ₹1,65,034 crore** to MSME vendors via the Samadhaan sub-portal from
  May 2020 to 5 Dec 2022 ✅ PIB — evidence the problem is recognised and large.
- **<1% of registered MSMEs ever file a delayed-payment application**, ≈1.3% of outstanding value
  — driven by *"fear of reprisal or loss of business"*. **Only ~20%** of filed applications are
  settled or disposed.
- ❌ **No startup-specific delayed-payment statistic exists.** Do not manufacture one; use the
  69% and 40% figures.

### The law already requires speed — and is ignored
**MSMED Act 2006 s.15** ✅ verbatim: *"in no case the period agreed upon… **shall exceed forty-five
days**."* Default absent a written agreement: **15 days**. **s.16:** **compound interest with
monthly rests at three times the RBI bank rate** — at the current 5.50% bank rate, **16.5% p.a.**
**s.43B(h) Income-tax Act** (from AY 2024-25) disallows the deduction entirely if an MSE is paid
late — and uniquely among s.43B clauses, **paying before the ITR due date does not save it**.
⚠️ **Medium enterprises and traders are excluded** from the delayed-payment chapter.

🔴 **MSMED (Amendment) Act 2026** — passed Rajya Sabha 3 Aug and Lok Sabha 7 Aug 2026.
**ss.15 and 16 are unchanged**; what changes is enforcement: new **s.15A** requires every **Central
Public Sector Enterprise to route invoice settlement through a RBI-authorised TReDS platform**;
mediation in 90 days, arbitration award in 90 days; awards recoverable **as arrears of land
revenue** and recognised as debt under the IBC. ⚠️ **Commencement not verified — treat as
passed-but-not-necessarily-in-force.**

### The sandbox that nobody could use
**SEBI's regulatory sandbox: 13 applications, 1 approved, in four years** (SEBI's own register,
26 Dec 2024). Versus **IRDAI's 173 + 185 applications** across its first two cohorts once entry
was genuinely open. **The constraint is never the absence of a sandbox — it is entry friction and
discretionary approval.**

**RBI's framework** ✅ (13 Aug 2019): five stages ≈ **27 weeks**; boundary conditions include
**caps on customer numbers, transaction ceilings and customer losses**; *"the RBI shall bear no
liability… any liability arising from the experiment will be borne by the applicant"*; indemnity
insurance running to **three months after exit**.
🎯 **ORF's criticism is Sanyog's opening:** *"The RBI does not provide any shared infrastructure
such as testing platforms, data access, or API environments."* **That missing shared
infrastructure is exactly what this platform is.**

## 6. Do NOT assert

### Corrections to the problem statement's own framing
1. ❌ **"GeM Green Channel" does not exist.** Green Channel Status is a **Ministry of Defence**
   policy. The GeM innovation route is **Startup Runway**.
2. ⚠️ **"Escrow-backed milestone payouts"** — payment escrow is not standard practice anywhere.
   **Deliverable/source-code escrow is.** See [international-precedents.md](international-precedents.md).
3. ⚠️ **"30% on onboarding"** is not a valid performance milestone (FAR 32.1004 bars payment on
   signature or passage of time). Reframe as a **secured mobilisation advance** — which sits
